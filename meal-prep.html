Meal Prep Cheatsheet

South-Indian leaning, reflux-friendly, dairy-free. 10 meals • 2 servings each • 10-min finish.

No tomato
No chili
Dairy-free
2 servings

Weekly Menu (10 meals)

1–2. Coconut curry + chicken + rice
3. Coconut curry + fish + rice
4. Coconut curry + chickpeas + rice
5–6. Keerai kootu + eggs + rice
7. Keerai kootu + chicken + rice
8–9. Moong dal + poriyal veg + rice
10. Moong dal + chickpeas + greens + rice

Batch Prep Quantities (20 servings)

Proteins
Chicken 4 lb • Fish 1.5 lb • Eggs 12–14 • Chickpeas 3 cans
Carb
Rice ~8 cups dry (≈24 cups cooked)
Veg
Spinach 16–20 oz • Onion 4–5 • Poriyal veg 3–4 lb
Pantry
Coconut milk 3 cans • Moong dal 3 cups • Cashews 1–1.5 cups

Base Sauces

Kerala Coconut Curry (4 meals): onion + ginger + garlic + cumin + coriander + turmeric + fennel + curry leaves + coconut milk.
Keerai Kootu (3 meals): spinach + cooked moong + coconut + cumin + garlic + mustard/urad/curry leaves tadka.
Moong Dal Base (3 meals): moong + water + turmeric + cumin + hing.

10-Min Finish (each meal)

1Sear protein 5–7 min.
2Add 1.5 cups base, simmer 2–3 min.
3Tadka: mustard + urad + curry leaves + garlic.
4Serve over reheated rice.

Appliance Cheats

Instant Pot: moong dal 10 min high + 10 min natural release.
Vitamix: blend curry bases smooth before final simmer.
Dutch oven: sweat onions gently (no browning).
Anova Oven: veg 425°F 15–20 min • chicken 375°F 18–22 min • fish 325–350°F 8–12 min.
